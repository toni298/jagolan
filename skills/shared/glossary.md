# Glossary

## Authentication

Verifies who the actor is.

## Authorization

Determines what the actor is allowed to do.

## Idempotency

Ensures repeated execution of the same logical operation does not create duplicate
side effects.

## Race Condition

A bug caused by timing or interleaving of concurrent operations.

## Transaction

A database unit of work that commits or rolls back as a whole.

## Observability

The ability to understand system behavior using logs, metrics, traces, and alerts.

## Blast Radius

The amount of damage a failure can cause.

## Reversibility

How easily a decision can be undone or migrated later.
